## Full Description

**Winter Forest** is a single-player first-person 3D wilderness survival game set in a quiet snowbound forest. Gather resources before nightfall, manage hunger, warmth, and health, return safely to your camp, and upgrade your shelter for longer expeditions.

**Core Survival Loop**
Cold intensifies at night, during snowfall, and across exposed ice. Gather firewood, berries, stone, cloth, pine resin, coal, iron, fish, and venison. Cook hot rations over the campfire, rest in your shelter, and level up your character to increase stamina and gathering efficiency.

**Five Distinct Regions & Unique Relics**
• **Base Clearing** — Safe starting camp with campfire, shelter, workbench, and the **Old Map**.
• **Dense Spruce Forest** — Heavy snowfall, abundant timber & pine resin, and the **Resin Torch**.
• **Frozen Lake** — Wind-swept glacial ice, fishing holes, and the **Insulated Thermos** base upgrade.
• **Abandoned Mine** — Dark timbered adits rich in coal and iron, home to the **Miner's Pickaxe**.
• **Hunter's Cabin** — Remote mountain pass cabin holding game meat, hides, and the **Warm Fur Coat**.

**Persistent Base Progression**
Upgrades to your Shelter, Campfire, and Workbench—along with permanent relics in your Collection of Findings—carry over across runs. Play offline anytime with local auto-saves on your device.
